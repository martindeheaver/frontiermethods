Our purpose
We don't just use technology. We develop the technologies that create new technological possibilities.

Our purpose is to identify, develop and commercialise breakthrough technologies across multiple scientific and engineering disciplines.

We use artificial intelligence to accelerate every step of that journey, so a small, focused team can take ideas from fundamental research to products, licences and new companies.

How we work

One engine, three stages

Each technology moves through the same path, from opportunity to working prototype to commercial outcome.

01 · Frontier Research
Discover

We look for opportunities in scientific research, patents, emerging technologies and unsolved engineering problems.

02 · Rapid Development
Develop

We use AI agents, simulation, software, automated design and rapid prototyping to turn promising ideas into working technology.

03 · Commercialisation
Commercialise

We validate, manufacture, license or spin out the resulting technologies as commercial businesses.

The stages reinforce one another. Every project leaves behind knowledge, infrastructure and methods that make the next one faster and cheaper, so Frontier Methods grows into a technology development engine rather than a collection of separate projects.

The role of AI

A small team with the reach of a large one

AI sits at the centre of how we operate. We use it to take on research and engineering that would once have needed a much larger organisation and far more time. Specialised AI agents support our team in:

  • Scientific literature and patent research
  • Finding promising research that has not been commercialised
  • Mathematical and computational modelling
  • Electronic circuit and hardware architecture design
  • Writing and testing software
  • Running simulations and analysing experimental results
  • Building prototypes and technical documentation
  • Finding manufacturing partners and routes to market
What AI does not replace. Physical experimentation, independent verification, specialist engineering judgement and regulatory validation are built into every project.
